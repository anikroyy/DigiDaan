How DigiDaan works

Four simple steps from a spare device to a student’s desk.

1. ListDonors add a working device and confirm it is wiped.
2. MatchCoordinators match it by urgency, fit and locality.
3. MeetHand over at the school or NGO, or in a public place.
4. VerifyThe donor sends a 4-digit code and the coordinator enters it to confirm the handover and make a receipt.

Questions

Who can donate?

Anyone with a working phone, laptop or tablet. It must power on and be factory reset first.

Is my number public?

No. Numbers are never shown on the site. They are used only to create message links after a match.

Who receives the devices?

Students whose need is registered by an approved school or NGO coordinator. Students appear by initials only.

How are schools and NGOs checked?

Each institution gives its registration ID (UDISE+ for schools, NGO Darpan for NGOs) and can be looked up on Google Maps. An admin approves it before its needs can be matched.

Is this a real service yet?

It is a college project prototype for Nirman Lab - I, so some listings may be demonstration data.
